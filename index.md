# Privacy Policy for GentleYarnCo Content Publisher

**Last updated: October 1, 2026**

GentleYarnCo Content Publisher is a private application used solely by the owner of GentleYarnCo to manage content for the GentleYarnCo Pinterest account.

The application may access the connected Pinterest account's basic profile information, boards, and Pins. This information is used to select destination boards, publish owner-approved Pins, and verify publication results.

Pinterest OAuth tokens and campaign information are stored locally on the owner's computer. The application does not collect Pinterest passwords.

Information accessed through Pinterest is not sold, rented, used for advertising, or shared with third parties.

The owner may revoke the application's Pinterest access and delete locally stored information at any time.

For privacy questions, contact the owner through the GentleYarnCo Etsy shop: [etsy.com/shop/GentleYarnCo](https://www.etsy.com/shop/GentleYarnCo)
